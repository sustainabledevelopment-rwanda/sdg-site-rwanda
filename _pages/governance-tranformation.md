The following list are outcomes for NST2 governance transformation pillar:

1. [Reduced case backlogs]({{ site.baseurl }}/NST2-gov-indicators-1/)

3. Quality and inclusive services are delivered and sustained
4. Enhanced transparency and accountability and compliance of PFM laws
5. Strengthened National unity and community resilience
6. Increased confidence in peace, personal and property security
