The following list are outcomes for NST2 social transformation pillar:

1. [Increased enrollment in pre-primary education]({{ site.baseurl }}/NST2-soc-indicators-1/)
2. Improved timely enrollment, efficiency, equity and learning outcomes in primary education
3. Increased net enrolment and quality in basic secondary education
4. Enhanced access to quality education in basic TVET
5. Reduced prevalence of stunting among under-five children
6. Reduced maternal and child mortality
7. Increased Human Resources for quality Health
8. Increased access to sanitation services
9. Enhanced empowerment of households to sustainably graduate out of poverty
10. Increased access to social security and income support programmes
