The following list are outcomes for NST2 economic transformation pillar:

- Sustainable economic growth and increased per capita incomes
- Increased national savings for investment
- Increased value of private investments
- Increased value of exports and reduced current account deficit
- Increased productive and decent jobs by 1.25 million (250,000 annually)
- Modernized Crop Production and productivity
- Strengthened Market Linkages and Post-Harvest Infrastructures
- Modernized Animal Resources production and productivity
- Strengthened Agriculture De-risking for resilience
- Increased sector contributions to exports
- Increased spatial access to electricity countrywide
- Enhanced share of renewable energy in electricity generation mix
- Increased access to drinking water
- Improved quality of the road network and trade supporting infrastructure
- Increased access to decent housing and enhanced resilience of communities
- Increased digital literacy
- Enhanced Environmental management, mitigation and adaptation to climate change
- Increased resilience to climate change and reduced impacts of disasters
- Mobilized climate and nature finance
