The following list are outcomes for NST2 economic transformation pillar:

1. [Sustainable economic growth and increased per capita incomes]({{ site.baseurl }}/NST2-eco-indicators-1/)
1. Increased national savings for investment
1. Increased value of private investments
1. Increased value of exports and reduced current account deficit
1. Increased productive and decent jobs by 1.25 million (250,000 annually)
1. Modernized Crop Production and productivity
1. Strengthened Market Linkages and Post-Harvest Infrastructures
1. Modernized Animal Resources production and productivity
1. Strengthened Agriculture De-risking for resilience
1. Increased sector contributions to exports
1. Increased spatial access to electricity countrywide
1. Enhanced share of renewable energy in electricity generation mix
1. Increased access to drinking water
1. Improved quality of the road network and trade supporting infrastructure
1. Increased access to decent housing and enhanced resilience of communities
1. Increased digital literacy
1. Enhanced Environmental management, mitigation and adaptation to climate change
1. Increased resilience to climate change and reduced impacts of disasters
1. Mobilized climate and nature finance
